What it is

Questions

What librarians usually ask

What's actually in the collection?

Scientific and medical imagery spanning anatomy and physiology, cell and molecular biology, microscopy, natural history, and the history of science. It's the kind of material instructors reach for to illustrate lectures, textbooks, and lab material.

Is this the same as your regular website?

It isn't. Licensed access runs through edu.sciencesource.com, a separate platform built for higher education. It offers full-resolution downloads, recognizes your campus network automatically, and includes a citation generator on every image and video page.

Who on campus is covered?

Every enrolled student and instructor is covered, across every department and course. It isn't a seat-limited login. Use extends to student coursework, projects, the LMS, slides, printed course packets, and other instructional material.

How is the price actually calculated?

Total FTE enrollment multiplied by the per-student rate, billed annually. There's no separate charge per image, per download, or per course.

Do we need to track individual image use?

That's not required. Once the license is active, faculty and students can use any image or video clip in the collection without submitting individual rights requests.
