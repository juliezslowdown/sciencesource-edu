What it is

Built for coursework, not just a catalog.

Science Source has spent decades building a library of scientific and medical imagery: anatomy and physiology, environmental science, cell and molecular biology, microscopy, natural history, and the history of science. In fact, your students and faculty have almost certainly encountered our images in major textbooks and their digital counterparts.

Now, students and instructors come to us directly to license imagery to illustrate lectures, slides, and course packets. The Education license simply streamlines that access, campus-wide, and frees the university community from worrying about one-off licensing and rights clearance.

Questions

What librarians usually ask

What's actually in the collection?

Scientific and medical imagery spanning anatomy and physiology, cell and molecular biology, microscopy, natural history, and the history of science: the kind of material instructors reach for to illustrate lectures, textbooks, and lab material.

Who on campus is covered?

Every enrolled student and instructor, across every department and course, not a seat-limited login. Use extends to the LMS, slides, printed course packets, and other instructional material.

How is the price actually calculated?

Total FTE enrollment multiplied by the per-student rate, billed annually. There's no separate charge per image, per download, or per course.

Do we need to track individual image use?

No. Once the license is active, faculty and students can use any image in the collection without submitting individual rights requests.

How long does setup take?

Once the agreement is signed, access opens the same day, with no separate technical rollout or account provisioning per user.
